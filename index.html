Math Quiz

How to play

  • You will get 7 math questions.
  • Each question has 4 answer choices. Click the one you think is right.
  • You have 20 seconds for each question. If time runs out, it counts as wrong.
  • A correct answer turns green. A wrong answer turns red.
  • Click Next to go to the next question.
  • Get at least 5 out of 7 correct to win. Fewer than 5 and you lose.
